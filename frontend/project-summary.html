What the product does

📍

Local weather intelligence

Tracks temperature, humidity, dew point, visibility, and recent conditions that contribute to black ice formation.

🛣️

Route-level awareness

Flags elevated risk around bridges, ramps, and colder corridors where black ice appears earlier than expected.

🧭

Actionable guidance

Turns raw weather data into practical next steps so users know when to slow down, avoid travel, or adjust route plans.

Why it matters

⚠️

Black ice is hard to see

It can form quickly on colder pavement, elevated road sections, and shaded surfaces, often before drivers realize the risk.

✅

Trust comes from clarity

Reliable winter safety tools need explainable conditions, risk levels, and practical recommendations people can act on immediately.

Built for communities, commuters, and local decision-makers who need to see risk sooner and act with more confidence during winter weather.
